# ResearchPilot home page brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation ("be creative", "use /scroll-craft for the ui and motion.dev").
Decisions below are authored, not quoted from an interview.

1. Vibe: exact, calm, checkable, editorial tool. References: a newspaper corrections column, a lab notebook, a code review.
2. Journey: the working input and your reports, then a sample run you drive by scrolling, then the input again.
3. Energy: quiet at the top, builds through read and write, loudest at verify, quiet at the close.
4. Feeling and the one moment: the visitor should feel they could audit the machine. The moment to remember is a
   confident sentence being struck out and removed because its source does not say it.
5. What no other site does: scrolling fact-checks a sentence in front of you, with a thread drawn to the passage.
6. Range: dense and tool-like, not premium-minimal marketing.
7. Structure: one surface that holds while its state advances (not separate scenes).
8. Assets: none. No photography; the product surface is the visual. Fonts: Geist and Geist Mono.

Grammar: live surface. The page is the app chrome (tab strip, status line), copy lives in labels and status text,
and the close is a real input. The other grammars lost because this is a tool whose honest pitch is "watch what it does".
Sample data is labelled "Sample run" and "illustrative data" on the surface.

Signature move: the evidence thread. An SVG path is drawn from each citation chip to the passage that backs it as the
scroll advances; the unsupported sentence gets a dashed red thread, a strike-through, and is removed.

Feeling curve
- Desk (input and reports): ready. A field and your own work, nothing to learn first.
- Plan: order. Outline rows arrive.
- Your review: control. One row is struck, one is added, by "you".
- Read: gathering. Source cards slide in.
- Write: momentum. Sentences appear with citation chips.
- Verify (peak, largest span): scrutiny, then relief. Threads connect, one sentence fails and disappears.
- Close: your turn. A command line waits.

Peak: "It deleted a sentence because the source did not back it." Lives in the verify stage.
Tell-someone sentence: It's the site where scrolling fact-checks a report and you watch a wrong sentence get removed.
Authored silence: none.

Not used from the skill: the scrollcraft engine (the request asked for motion.dev, so scroll linking uses Motion's
`scroll()`), generated photography (no key, and the grammar bans it), dimensional photo layering (not applicable to
a live surface).
