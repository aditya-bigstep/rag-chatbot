Sheet P-2 · Ledger

A chatbot answer gives you nothing to check.

What you get from a plain chatbot answer and from ResearchPilot
QuestionPlain chatbotResearchPilot
Where did this fact come from?No sources, or links it made upEvery sentence cites a page that was actually downloaded
Does the source really say that?You check by handEach sentence is checked against its source. Click a citation to see the passage
Who decides the structure?The modelYou approve or edit the outline before anything is written
What if one section is weak?Ask again, get a different answerOnly that section is redone. The rest stays word for word
Sentences with no citation, per report1.9 in our test without the checker0 with the checker, across 7 test topics

So the rule is short: no citation, no claim. A sentence stays only if a source backs it.
